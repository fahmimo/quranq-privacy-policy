Account Deletion Request

Privacy Terms Delete Account

At QuranQ, we respect your data ownership. You have the full right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time directly through the application.

Warning: Irreversible Action

Deleting your account is permanent. Once completed:

  • Your profile and learning progress will be wiped.
  • Your unique username will be released for others.
  • Your community posts and achievements will be deleted.
  • This action cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

To comply with Apple's security requirements, account deletion must be initiated from within the authenticated application:

1
Open QuranQ App Launch the application on your iOS or Android device.
2
Go to Settings Tap the Profile tab, then select the Settings icon (gear) at the top right.
3
Find "Hapus Akun" Scroll to the bottom of the Settings menu and tap on "Hapus Akun secara Permanen" (Permanently Delete Account).
4
Confirm Deletion A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap "Hapus" to proceed with the permanent deletion.

Data Retention Policy

Upon clicking the delete button, your data is removed from our live databases (Cloud Firestore) immediately. Backup snapshots may persist in our encrypted backup systems for up to 30 days before being purged automatically, strictly for disaster recovery purposes.

Need Assistance?

If you have lost access to your device and need to request deletion manually, please contact us via our support email mentioning your registered username/email.
