Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy Terms of Service
Last Updated: April 19, 2026

QuranQ ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to providing a safe and transparent Quran learning experience. This policy explains how we manage your information with a Privacy-First principle.

1. Data We Collect

Our application collects limited information necessary for core functionality:

  • Profile Data: Display name, gender, and avatar (emoji or image URL) for community identity.
  • Progress Data: Quiz scores, unlocked Juz, and achievements for learning synchronization.
  • Community Data: Posts or check-ins that you voluntarily share in the Global Activity Feed.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is strictly used to:

  • Synchronize data across your devices so you never lose your progress.
  • Provide interactive features such as Leaderboards and Community Feeds.
  • Process Premium subscription transactions through official platform systems (App Store/Play Store).

3. Protection & Security

We use Firebase by Google, an industry-standard service, to store data. All data is transmitted over secure protocols (SSL/TLS), and we never sell your personal data to third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Control & Deletion

In accordance with our commitment to Apple and Google compliance:

  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data via the Settings > Delete Account menu within the app.
  • This action is immediate and will permanently remove your profile, username, and cloud data.

5. Contact Us

Questions about your privacy?

Contact us directly via the Help & Feedback feature inside the QuranQ app.
